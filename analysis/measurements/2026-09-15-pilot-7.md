## 5. Defects found

**D-7 (new). A turn that raises records neither its authorisation nor its
settlement.** `generate_task._turn` builds `turn = {"usage": {}, ...}` before
dispatching and only rebinds it on return, so when `dispatch_turn` raises the
`finally` writes the placeholder: `llm_seed_read.usage.json` is `{}` and the
ledger row for `seeded/stage_1` carries `authorised_usd`, `ceiling_usd`,
`billed_usd` and `calls` all null. The guard's own settlement is correct and
conservative — an unobserved turn keeps its whole worst case — but it happens
in the generator's process and dies with it, so a raising turn is invisible to
`ledger.aggregate`, which then counts it at nothing. Pilot 6 had the same hole
and could not see it, its turn having been refused BEFORE dispatch. The fix is
to carry `authorised_usd` and `settled_usd` out of `dispatch_turn` on the
raising path too, which means the exception must reach `_turn` with the numbers
attached rather than bare.

**D-8 (new, environmental). The express-mode quota refused the first call.**
Pilot 6's stage 1 spent 768,262 input tokens at 19:17Z and pilot 7's first call
at 19:42Z was refused as exhausted; pilot 5's replayed turn answered HTTP 429
the same way. Nothing in this repository can change that, and what it costs is
measurement time rather than money.

## 6. What this does not claim

That the guard settles correctly in a live run. It is exercised by tests
against pilot 6's own numbers — authorise 2.729162, settle 0.605893, authorise
4.384600 admitted inside a cap of 6.00 — and live only in the negative sense
that the authorisation happened and nothing was billed.
