## 4. Defects found

**D-1 (new). The forced final answer returns no text.** The 13th call produced
neither a text part nor a thought part the stream could show. Two hypotheses,
neither tested — no live retry was made:

  a. dropping `tools` from the config while the conversation history carries
     `function_call` and `function_response` parts leaves the request
     inconsistent, and the model answers with nothing. The fix that keeps the
     history valid is to KEEP the declarations and forbid a call instead
     (`tool_config` with the function-calling mode set to NONE) rather than to
     remove `tools`;
  b. the final call's output budget went to thinking (8,165 of the turn's 9,027
     output tokens are thought) and the response was truncated before any text.
     `finish_reason` is not read on the final-answer path, so a `MAX_TOKENS`
     there is recorded as "empty" rather than as truncation.

Distinguishing a from b needs the 13th call's own `finish_reason` and part
list, neither of which the current stream records.

**D-2 (new). USD 3.00 admits exactly one tool-bearing turn at a cap of 12.** The
pre-authorisation prices one stage-1 turn at 2.729162: thirteen calls, each one
carrying the prompt plus up to `TOOL_OUTPUT_TOKENS_CAP` per prior tool result,
plus 13 x 16,000 output tokens. Two of those do not fit under 3.00, so W1
refused the second turn before dispatch, the arm stopped on
`campaign_spend_cap`, and the mutation arm never started. The registered
per-stage cap and the registered spend cap are not independent: a pilot that
wants n turns needs a cap of at least n x 2.73 at `stage_1: 12`. The run is
bounded as designed; the pilot's own cap was the binding constraint, not the
model's behaviour.

**D-3 (new). A run that stops before its second arm renders no results
artefact.** `results/results_refused.txt`: "both arms' windows are printed
beside the headline and no arm_window entry exists for ['mutation']". The
campaign wrote its counters and its ledger, and then refused to render the
Markdown, so a stopped run reports through the database only.
