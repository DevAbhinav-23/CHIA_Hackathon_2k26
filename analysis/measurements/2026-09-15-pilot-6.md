## 5. Defects found

**D-5 (new). An authorisation is never reconciled to what the turn cost.**
`SpendGuard.authorise` adds the worst case to `authorised_usd` and nothing
subtracts the difference when the turn comes back cheaper. Stage 1 was
authorised 2.729162 and billed 0.605893 — 22.2 % of it — and the remaining
2.123269 stayed held. With it released, the headroom at stage 2 would have been
5.394107 against a worst case of 4.384600 and the turn would have been sent.
This is not new code: the same accumulation refused pilot 3's second turn at a
cap of 3.00. What is new is that seventeen priced calls instead of thirteen
raised stage 2's worst case by about USD 1.67, which made a cap that fitted two
turns fit one. Either the guard releases the unspent balance when a turn returns
its usage, or the pilot's cap must exceed 7.113762; the first is the honest fix,
because the cap is supposed to bound money SPENT.

**D-6 (new, fixed). The turn-failure caption did not agree with its count.**
The artefact printed "1 turn produced no probing input at all and so appear in
no other table". Found by reading this run's own `results.md`; the sentence is
now split at the count (commit 056773a), the fixture re-rendered and the
assertion pinned to the full stop.
