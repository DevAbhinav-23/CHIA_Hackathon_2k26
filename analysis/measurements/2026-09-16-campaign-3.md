## The throttling anomaly

Vertex returned 429s throughout. Shard 1 wrote no new probe for over two hours, about 10:00 to 12:20 IST, while turns kept being billed
against it. The backend's retry ladder reached its last rung, 6 of 6, and some turns were abandoned after three outer attempts with the
exception masked behind a TaskGroup wrapper, so the run log shows a turn ending without a cause. Documented here, not fixed.
Throughput across the two shards over the 11 h 53 min was 43 seeds, about 3.6 seeds an hour.
